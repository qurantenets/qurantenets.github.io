    The Prophet disowned those who wail, shave their heads, slap cheeks or tear clothes; grieving Zaid, Ja'far and Ibn Rawaha, he had Ja'far's crying women forbidden.

    The Prophet wept for his son Ibrahim and, beside the sick Sa'd bin Ubada, said Allah punishes for the tongue, not tears; only five women kept the pledge against wailing.
