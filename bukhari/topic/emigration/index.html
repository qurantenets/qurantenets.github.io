Emigration

62 hadith · 17 flows · in 10 books

#TopicHadithBookCount
  1. 636

    When permission to emigrate came, the Prophet told Abu Bakr at noon, accepted his company, and took one of his two prepared she-camels only on paying its price.

    2138Sales and Trade1
  2. 1102

    No migration remains after the Conquest of Mecca, only jihad and good intention; the Prophet takes Mujalid's pledge for Islam instead, and Aisha says the same.

    3077–3080Fighting for the Cause of Allah (Jihaad)4
  3. 1343

    On the Migration Abu Bakr made the Prophet a bed in the shade and brought him a shepherd's milk; Suraqa's horse sank until he promised to turn back pursuers.

    3615Virtues and Merits of the Prophet (pbuh) and his Companions1
  4. 1360

    Fleeing Mecca, Abu Bakr made the Prophet a bed in the shade and brought him a shepherd's milk; in the cave the Prophet said Allah was their third.

    3652–3653Companions of the Prophet2
  5. 1412

    On the emigrants to Ethiopia: the Prophet condemns pictures and worship places over graves seen there, no longer answers greetings in prayer, and gives the ship's people two migrations.

    3873–3876Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)4
  6. 1419

    Khabbab says the emigrants' reward is with Allah, recalling Mus'ab's short shroud; emigration counts by intention, ended with Mecca's conquest, and the Prophet lived ten years as an emigrant.

    3897–3903Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)7
  7. 1421

    Abu Bakr gives up Ibn ad-Daghina's protection and migrates with the Prophet via Thaur's cave; Suraqa's horse sinks; Asma's son is born at Quba; Abdullah bin Salam accepts Islam.

    3905–3911Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)7
  8. 1422

    Umar's grants to early Emigrants; Khabbab on Emigrants who died unrewarded in this world, like Mus'ab; Umar's hope for their deeds with the Prophet; Ibn Umar pledging before his father.

    3912–3916Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)5
  9. 1423

    Abu Bakr recounts the journey, milk from a shepherd and hiding in the cave; he kisses feverish Aisha, dyes his hair, divorces Um Bakr; a bedouin asks about migrating.

    3917–3923Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)7
  10. 1424

    The first Emigrants and the Prophet reach Medina; he prays for its love and health; Uthman's two migrations; Medina, place of migration and Sunna; Ansar lodge Emigrants by lot.

    3924–3929Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)6
  11. 1426

    The Prophet's mosque is built; Emigrants get three days in Mecca after Hajj; his arrival starts the calendar; prayer becomes four rak'at except travelling; he prays their migration be fulfilled.

    3932–3936Merits of the Helpers in Madinah (Ansaar)5
  12. 1505

    After the Conquest the Prophet takes pledges for Islam and jihad, not migration; Ibn Umar and Aisha say migration has ended, though jihad and intention remain, as Islam now prevails.

    4305–4312Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi)8
  13. 1829

    Al-Bara lists the first emigrants to reach Medina, who taught the Quran there, then Umar with twenty men, and the city's joy at the Prophet's arrival.

    4941Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh))1
  14. 1935

    The Prophet tested believing women who came as emigrants, as Allah ordered, and accepted their pledge of allegiance by word alone, his hand never touching a woman's.

    5288Divorce1
  15. 2089

    Allowed to leave Mecca, the Prophet came to Abu Bakr at midday, head covered; Asma tied their food bag with her girdle, and they stayed three nights in Thour's cave.

    5807Dress1
  16. 2186

    The Prophet visited Abu Bakr's house morning and evening, and one day came at noon to say he had been allowed to migrate.

    6079Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab)1
  17. 2222

    A bedouin asking about emigration was told it is hard, but since he paid Zakat on his camels, he could carry on from beyond the seas, losing no deed.

    6165Good Manners and Form (Al-Adab)1
