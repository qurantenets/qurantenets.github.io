Halal & Haram

67 hadith · 16 flows · in 11 books

#TopicHadithBookCount
  1. 36

    The lawful and the unlawful are clear with doubtful things between; avoiding these protects religion and honour, Allah's forbidden things are His reserve, and the heart decides the body's soundness.

    52Belief1
  2. 606

    Between clear lawful and unlawful lie doubts: the Prophet was wary of a claimed foster marriage, doubtful game and stray dates, but doubt breaks no ablution and forbids no meat.

    2051–2057Sales and Trade7
  3. 662

    Passing a dead sheep, the Prophet says its skin could be used, for only eating it is unlawful.

    2221Sales and Trade1
  4. 664

    Umar curses a seller of alcohol, citing the curse on Jews who sold fat forbidden to them; picture-makers will be punished, and the alcohol trade was made unlawful.

    2223–2226Sales and Trade4
  5. 670

    In Mecca's conquest year the Prophet forbids trading alcohol, carcasses, pigs and idols; he forbade a dog's price, money for blood, prostitutes' and soothsayers' earnings, and cursed tattooing and Riba.

    2236–2238Sales and Trade3
  6. 690

    The Prophet forbade the price of a dog, the earnings of prostitutes and slave-girls by prostitution, a soothsayer's charges, and payment for an animal's copulation.

    2282–2284Hiring3
  7. 749

    Allah forbids undutifulness to mothers, burying daughters alive, withholding others' rights and begging, and dislikes idle talk, too many questions and wasting wealth.

    2408Loans, Payment of Loans, Freezing of Property, Bankruptcy1
  8. 794

    At Khaibar the Prophet ordered the pots cooking donkey meat to be emptied and broken, then allowed them to be washed instead.

    2477Oppressions1
  9. 1482

    At Khaibar the Prophet forbids donkey meat, as well as garlic and temporary marriage, has the pots overturned and allows horse meat; companions differ over the reason for the ban.

    4214–4227Military Expeditions led by the Prophet (pbuh) (Al-Maghaazi)14
  10. 1650

    Men on campaign without wives asked about castrating themselves; the Prophet forbade it, let them marry a woman temporarily for a garment, and recited the verse against forbidding good things.

    4615Prophetic Commentary on the Qur'an (Tafseer of the Prophet (pbuh))1
  11. 1957

    The Prophet forbade the price of a dog, a soothsayer's fee and earnings from prostitution, including a slave girl's, and cursed tattooing, taking or giving riba, and picture-makers.

    5346–5348Divorce3
  12. 2013

    Troops ate a fish the sea cast up, and locusts; People of the Scripture's vessels are used, washed, only if no others exist; domestic donkey meat at Khaibar was discarded.

    5493–5497Hunting, Slaughtering5
  13. 2022

    Donkey meat and Mut'a marriage were forbidden at Khaibar, horse meat allowed, though Ibn Abbas withheld a verdict on donkeys; fanged beasts are forbidden; a dead sheep's hide is usable.

    5519–5532Hunting, Slaughtering14
  14. 2025

    The Prophet accepted a rabbit's legs and neither ate nor forbade mastigure, unknown in his land; butter-fat is eaten once a dead mouse and the fat around it are discarded.

    5535–5540Hunting, Slaughtering6
  15. 2673

    When companions stopped eating meat on hearing it was mastigure, the Prophet told them to carry on, for it is lawful, though not among his own foods.

    7267Accepting Information Given by a Truthful Person1
  16. 2747

    Game caught by trained dogs sent with Allah's Name may be eaten, as may meat brought by new Muslims when one mentions Allah's Name over it oneself.
