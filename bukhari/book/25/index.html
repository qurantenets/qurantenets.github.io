Hajj (Pilgrimage)

38 flows · 100% of hadith grouped · hadith Nos. 1513–1772

#TopicHadithCount
  1. 512

    At the Farewell Pilgrimage the Prophet let a Khath'am woman perform the obligatory Hajj for her old father, who could not sit a mount, and turned Al-Fadl's face from her.

    1513Hajj (Pilgrimage)1
  2. 513

    The Prophet began saying Labbaik at Dhul-Hulaifa once his mount stood up carrying him.

    1514–1515Hajj (Pilgrimage)2
  3. 514

    The Prophet and Anas made Hajj on a packsaddle and Aisha rode one to Umrah; after faith and jihad Hajj Mabrur is best, women's best jihad, wiping away sins.

    1516–1521Hajj (Pilgrimage)6
  4. 515

    The Prophet fixed Qarn for the people of Najd, Dhul-Hulaifa for Medina and Al-Juhfa for Sham as the places to assume Ihram.

    1522Hajj (Pilgrimage)1
  5. 516

    Yemenis came for Hajj without provisions, saying they relied on Allah, and begged in Medina, so Allah revealed that they should take provision, the best provision being fear of Allah.

    1523Hajj (Pilgrimage)1
  6. 517

    The Prophet fixed Dhul-Hulaifa, Al-Juhfa, Qarn and Yalamlam as Miqat for Medina, Sham, Najd, Yemen and passers-by, those within assuming Ihram at home; Umar added Dhatu-Irq.

    1524–1531Hajj (Pilgrimage)8
  7. 518

    The Prophet prayed at Dhul-Hulaifa, went by Ash-Shajara and returned by Muarras, and was told Al-Aqiq is a blessed valley for prayer and Ihram of Hajj and Umrah.

    1532–1535Hajj (Pilgrimage)4
  8. 519

    Perfume before and during ihram, the Prophet's matted hair, his assuming ihram at the mosque of Dhul-Hulaifa, and the garments a muhrim may not wear.

    1536–1542Hajj (Pilgrimage)7
  9. 520

    The Talbiya: its words, begun when the Prophet's mount stood up after praying at Dhul-Hulaifa, said aloud, kept up until stoning Jamrat al-Aqaba, and the Prophet picturing Moses reciting it.

    1543–1555Hajj (Pilgrimage)13
  10. 521

    In the Prophet's last Hajj, those with a sacrificial animal kept ihram until Hajj ended and others left it after Umrah; Ali and Abu Musa took his intention.

    1556–1562Hajj (Pilgrimage)7
  11. 522

    Hajj-at-Tamattu and Qiran: Ali kept to them despite Uthman, pre-Islamic people shunned Umrah in Hajj months, and those without a sacrificial animal left ihram, owing sacrifice or fasting.

    1563–1572Hajj (Pilgrimage)10
  12. 523

    The Prophet spent the night at Dhi-Tuwa and bathed before entering Mecca, entering from its higher side at Kada' and leaving from its lower side.

    1573–1581Hajj (Pilgrimage)9
  13. 524

    The Prophet carried stones for the Ka'bah; Quraysh rebuilt it short of Abraham's foundations, which he would have restored but for his people's recent ignorance, and Ibn Az-Zubair later did.

    1582–1586Hajj (Pilgrimage)5
  14. 525

    Mecca is Allah's sanctuary; Aqil and Talib, not Ja'far and Ali, inherited Abu Talib's property; the Prophet would stay at Khaif Bani Kinana, where Quraysh had pledged against Bani Hashim.

    1587–1590Hajj (Pilgrimage)4
  15. 526

    An Ethiopian will demolish the Ka'bah, Hajj to it outlasts Gog and Magog, its cover and treasure, Umar kissing the Black Stone, and the Prophet praying or saying Takbir inside.

    1591–1601Hajj (Pilgrimage)11
  16. 527

    Ramal in the first three rounds, begun to show the pagans strength, and touching or kissing only the Black Stone and Yemeni corners, or pointing at the Corner when riding.

    1602–1613Hajj (Pilgrimage)12
  17. 528

    On reaching Mecca the Prophet made ablution and began with Tawaf; women circled apart from men, the sick rode, a man's leading rope was cut, and naked Tawaf was banned.

    1614–1622Hajj (Pilgrimage)9
  18. 529

    After seven rounds, two rak'at at Maqam Ibrahim, then Safa and Marwa, with no marital relations before that; Tawaf after the morning prayer, avoiding forbidden times, and circling while riding.

    1623–1633Hajj (Pilgrimage)11
  19. 530

    Al-Abbas could stay in Mecca on the nights of Mina to water the pilgrims; the Prophet drank Zamzam, praised those drawing it, and Jibril washed his chest with it.

    1634–1637Hajj (Pilgrimage)4
  20. 531

    Those combining Hajj and Umrah, like the Prophet and Ibn Umar, began with Tawaf of the Ka'bah and did a single Tawaf between Safa and Marwa for both.

    1638–1642Hajj (Pilgrimage)5
  21. 532

    Going between Safa and Marwa may not be left: Aisha and Anas explain the verse calling them Allah's symbols, the Prophet ran there, and relations wait until it is done.

    1643–1649Hajj (Pilgrimage)7
  22. 533

    A menstruating woman performs every Hajj rite except Tawaf of the Ka'bah until she is clean, as Aisha did; menstruating women attend Arafat but keep away from the prayer place.

    1650–1652Hajj (Pilgrimage)3
  23. 534

    The Prophet prayed the noon and afternoon prayers at Mina on the day of Tarwiya, and he, Abu Bakr, Umar and Uthman early on prayed two rak'at at Mina.

    1653–1657Hajj (Pilgrimage)5
  24. 535

    On the day of Arafat the Prophet was seen not fasting, pilgrims said Talbiya or Takbir, set out at midday after a short sermon, and the Hums were sent there.

    1658–1665Hajj (Pilgrimage)8
  25. 536

    Leaving Arafat at a calm pace, the Prophet made light ablution at a pass, saying the prayer lay ahead, then joined the sunset and night prayers at Muzdalifah.

    1666–1675Hajj (Pilgrimage)10
  26. 537

    The weak and women could leave Muzdalifah by night; the Prophet prayed Fajr there at first dawn, left before sunrise unlike the pagans, and said Talbiya until stoning Jamrat al-Aqaba.

    1676–1687Hajj (Pilgrimage)12
  27. 538

    The sacrifice owed for Tamattu, a camel, cow or sheep or else fasting; riding one's sacrificial camel; and the Prophet and Ibn Umar driving sacrificial animals for Hajj with Umrah.

    1688–1693Hajj (Pilgrimage)6
  28. 539

    Garlanding and marking sacrificial animals, Aisha twisting their garlands, the Prophet sending them, sheep included, from Medina without entering ihram, riding a sacrificial camel, and giving their skins in charity.

    1694–1707Hajj (Pilgrimage)14
  29. 540

    Slaughtering at the Prophet's place at Mina, camels standing, cows for his wives, meat, skins and covers given in charity, none as butcher's wages, and keeping meat past three days.

    1708–1720Hajj (Pilgrimage)13
  30. 541

    Day of Nahr rites: no harm in changing their order, ihram kept until the sacrifice, the Prophet praying for those who shave before those who shorten, and Tawaf al-Ifada.

    1721–1733Hajj (Pilgrimage)13
  31. 542

    Asked at Mina on the Day of Nahr about shaving, slaughtering or stoning done out of order, the Prophet told each questioner: do it now, there is no harm.

    1734–1738Hajj (Pilgrimage)5
  32. 543

    The Day of Nahr sermon: blood, property and honour are as sacred as that day, month and town; the present must tell the absent; do not strike one another's necks.

    1739–1742Hajj (Pilgrimage)4
  33. 544

    Those watering the pilgrims could spend Mina's nights in Mecca; the pillars are stoned after midday, from the valley's middle, seven pebbles with Takbir, supplicating after the first two.

    1743–1753Hajj (Pilgrimage)11
  34. 545

    Aisha perfumed the Prophet before Tawaf al-Ifada; pilgrims make Tawaf their last act before leaving Mecca, menstruating women excused, and the Prophet circled after resting at Al-Muhassab.

    1754–1756Hajj (Pilgrimage)3
  35. 546

    A woman whose menses begin after Tawaf al-Ifada may leave without waiting, as the Prophet allowed Safiya; Um Sulaim's report backs Ibn Abbas's ruling, and Aisha performs Umrah from Tan'im.

    1757–1762Hajj (Pilgrimage)6
  36. 547

    On leaving Mina the Prophet prayed and slept at Al-Muhassab, a camping place rather than a rite, and Ibn Umar spent the night at Dhi-Tuwa on entering and leaving Mecca.

    1763–1769Hajj (Pilgrimage)7
  37. 548

    Muslims avoided trading at the old markets of Dhul-Majaz and Ukaz until a verse allowed seeking their Lord's bounty during Hajj.

    1770Hajj (Pilgrimage)1
  38. 549

    Safiya, menstruating on the night of departure, was allowed to leave because she had done Tawaf al-Ifada, and Aisha performed Umrah from Tan'im.

    1771–1772Hajj (Pilgrimage)2
