Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)

22 flows · 100% of hadith grouped · hadith Nos. 6861–6917

#TopicHadithCount
  1. 2498

    The greatest sins are setting up a rival to Allah, killing one's son lest he share one's food, and adultery with a neighbour's wife, as a revealed verse confirms.

    6861Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1

    A believer remains at liberty in his religion unless he kills unlawfully, bloodshed is the first case judged, and Al-Miqdad was told not to kill a foe who declared Islam.

    Every unjust killing lays a share of the guilt on Adam's first son, who began the practice of murder on earth.

    6867Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1

    The Prophet warned, also at the Farewell Hajj, that after him they must not turn disbelievers by striking one another's necks.

    6868–6869Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)2

    The biggest sins are joining partners with Allah, undutifulness to parents, murder, and a false oath, statement or witness.

    6870–6871Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)2
  6. 2503

    Usama was rebuked for killing a man who said none is worshipped but Allah; the pledge forbade killing, and two Muslims fighting with swords are both in the Fire.

    6872–6875Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)4
  7. 2504

    A Jew who killed a girl was killed with stones; a Muslim's blood is lawful only in three cases, and the victim's relative may choose blood money or retaliation.

    6876–6881Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)6
  8. 2505

    The three most hated to Allah are an evildoer in the sanctuary, one seeking pre-Islamic ways in Islam, and one seeking to shed blood without right.

    6882Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1
  9. 2506

    At Uhud, after Satan's shout, the army's front ranks attacked their own rear and killed Al-Yaman despite his son Hudhaifa's cries, and Hudhaifa asked Allah to forgive them.

    6883Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1
  10. 2507

    A Jew who crushed a girl's head was killed with stones, and the Prophet, dosed against his wish, said all but Al-Abbas would be made to drink medicine.

    6884–6886Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)3
  11. 2508

    No blame falls on one who blinds with a stone a man peeping into his house without permission, and the Prophet aimed an arrowhead at a man peeping in.

    6887–6889Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)3
  12. 2509

    At Uhud, after Satan's shout, Muslims killed Al-Yaman as his son Hudhaifa cried out, and Hudhaifa asked Allah to forgive the killer until he died.

    6890Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1
  13. 2510

    Some said Amir, killed at Khaibar, lost his deeds by killing himself, but the Prophet said whoever says so lies, for Amir has a double reward.

    6891Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1
  14. 2511

    No blood money for a biter's lost teeth, but retaliation for a broken tooth, slaps and a group killing; finger equals thumb; those who dosed the Prophet must drink it.

    6892–6897Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)7
  15. 2512

    With no proof against the killer of a man found slain at Khaibar, the Prophet paid the blood money, and Abu Qilaba argued against basing retaliation on qasama oaths.

    6898–6899Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)2
  16. 2513

    The Prophet aimed an arrowhead at a man peeping into his house, said asking permission exists to prevent unlawful looks, and one who pokes a peeper's eye is blameless.

    6900–6902Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)3
  17. 2514

    Ali's sheet held blood-money rules; the Prophet ruled a male or female slave for a miscarried fetus, as Al-Mughira told Umar, and the killer's relatives paid for the slain woman.

    6903–6910Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)9
  18. 2515

    Anas, brought as a boy to serve the Prophet at home and on journeys, said the Prophet never asked him why he had or had not done anything.

    6911Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1
  19. 2516

    No blood money is due for one killed by an animal, a well or a mine, and a fifth of buried treasure goes to the state.

    6912–6913Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)2
  20. 2517

    Whoever kills a person under the Muslims' pledge of protection will not smell Paradise's fragrance, though it reaches across forty years' travel.

    6914Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1

    6915Blood Money (Ad-Diyat)1
  22. 2519
