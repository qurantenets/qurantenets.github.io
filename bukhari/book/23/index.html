Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)

47 flows · 100% of hadith grouped · hadith Nos. 1237–1394

#TopicHadithCount
  1. 436

    Whoever dies worshipping none besides Allah enters Paradise, even after adultery and theft, and whoever dies worshipping others besides Him enters the Fire.

    1237–1238Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  2. 437

    A Muslim's rights on Muslims include returning greetings, visiting the sick, following funerals, accepting invitations and answering sneezers; silver vessels, gold rings and silk were forbidden.

    1239–1240Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  3. 438

    Abu Bakr kissed the dead Prophet, weeping, then told the people that whoever worshipped Muhammad, he is dead, but Allah is alive, and recited 3:144.

    1241–1242Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  4. 439

    The Prophet questioned a claim that Allah honoured the dead Uthman bin Maz'un, allowed weeping over a martyr, prayed for An-Najashi, wept announcing three martyrs, and prayed at a grave.

    1243–1247Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  5. 440

    A Muslim whose three children, or even two, die before puberty is granted Paradise and screened from the Fire.

    1248–1251Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)4
  6. 441

    The Prophet told a woman weeping beside a grave to fear Allah and be patient.

    1252Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  7. 442

    The Prophet had his dead daughter washed an odd number of times with water and Sidr, from the right side, with camphor last, and shrouded in his waist-sheet.

    1253–1258Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)6
  8. 443

    The Prophet had his dead daughter washed three, five or more times with water and Sidr, camphor last, and shrouded in his waist-sheet; her hair was put in three braids.

    1259–1263Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  9. 444

    The Prophet was shrouded in three white cotton sheets; a pilgrim killed by his mount at Arafat was shrouded in two sheets, unperfumed and bareheaded, to rise saying Labbaik.

    1264–1268Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  10. 445

    The Prophet gave his shirt to shroud Abdullah bin Ubai and prayed over him despite Umar's objection; a verse then forbade praying over dead hypocrites.

    1269–1270Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  11. 446

    The Prophet was shrouded in three cotton sheets; Abdur-Rahman wept that Mus'ab and Hamza had only a Burd as shroud; a man asked for the Prophet's cloak as his shroud.

    1271–1277Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)7
  12. 447

    Women were forbidden, not strictly, to follow funerals, and may mourn no one over three days except a husband, four months and ten days, as Um Habiba and Zainab taught.

    1278–1282Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  13. 448

    The Prophet told a woman weeping at a grave that patience is at the first stroke of a calamity, urged his daughter to be patient, and called his tears mercy.

    1283–1284Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  14. 449

    The Prophet wept at his daughter's grave; Umar said the dead are punished for relatives' wailing, which Aisha limited to disbelievers; slapping cheeks and tearing clothes are condemned.

    1285–1294Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)10
  15. 450

    Visiting the sick Sa'd, whose only heir was a daughter, the Prophet limited his bequest in charity to one third, heirs being better left rich, and pitied Sa'd bin Khaula.

    1295Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  16. 451

    The Prophet disowned those who wail, shave their heads, slap cheeks or tear clothes; grieving Zaid, Ja'far and Ibn Rawaha, he had Ja'far's crying women forbidden.

    1296–1300Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  17. 452

    Abu Talha's wife hid their son's death from him until morning, and the Prophet prayed that their night be blessed; real patience is at the first stroke of a calamity.

    1301–1302Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  18. 453

    The Prophet wept for his son Ibrahim and, beside the sick Sa'd bin Ubada, said Allah punishes for the tongue, not tears; only five women kept the pledge against wailing.

    1303–1306Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)4
  19. 454

    Stand for a passing funeral procession, even a Jew's, until it passes or is set down, and do not sit before it is down; carry the dead quickly.

    1307–1316Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)10
  20. 455

    The Prophet led An-Najashi's funeral prayer in rows with four Takbir and prayed at a grave of one buried at night; attending a funeral prayer earns a Qirat, burial two.

    1317–1328Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)12
  21. 456

    The Prophet ordered a Jewish man and woman, brought to him for illegal sexual intercourse, to be stoned near the place of funeral prayers beside the mosque.

    1329Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  22. 457

    Allah cursed Jews and Christians for praying at their prophets' graves; the Prophet stood by a dead woman's middle, said four Takbir and prayed at graves; Al-Fatiha is recited.

    1330–1337Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)8
  23. 458

    Two angels ask the newly buried about the Prophet; the believer is shown his place in Paradise, while the disbeliever or hypocrite is struck with an iron hammer.

    1338Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  24. 459

    Moses slapped the angel of death; offered years as many as the hairs under his hand on an ox, he chose death then, near the Sacred Land.

    1339Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  25. 460

    The Prophet prayed over a man buried at night, called those who build places of worship with pictures at graves the worst creatures, and had Abu Talha bury his daughter.

    1340–1342Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)3
  26. 461

    Uhud's martyrs were buried in pairs, the one knowing more Quran first, in their blood, unwashed and unprayed for; another report says the Prophet prayed for them.

    1343–1348Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)6
  27. 462

    Allah made Mecca a sanctuary where none may cut its plants, chase its game or pick up lost things except to announce them, idhkhir excepted for graves, goldsmiths and houses.

    1349Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  28. 463

    The Prophet had the dead Abdullah bin Ubai taken from his grave and clothed in the Prophet's shirt, in return, people thought, for Ibn Ubai's shirt given to Al-Abbas.

    1350Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  29. 464

    Jabir moved his father, Uhud's first martyr, to a separate grave after six months, barely changed; the Prophet buried Uhud's martyrs two to a grave, unwashed, in their blood.

    1351–1353Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)3
  30. 465

    The Prophet tested the boy Ibn Saiyad and refused Umar leave to kill him, saying the Dajjal could not be overpowered; he later tried to overhear him.

    1354–1355Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  31. 466

    A sick Jewish boy serving the Prophet embraced Islam; Ibn Abbas was a child among the weak; every child is born with true faith and receives the funeral prayer.

    1356–1359Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)4
  32. 467

    Dying, Abu Talib refused the Prophet's call to declare Allah's oneness and, urged by Abu Jahl, kept Abdul Muttalib's religion; a verse forbade seeking forgiveness for pagans.

    1360Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  33. 468

    Passing two graves, the Prophet said one man was punished for carelessness with urine, the other for slander, and fixed a green palm leaf on each to lessen it.

    1361Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  34. 469

    At a funeral in Baqi the Prophet said every soul's place in Paradise or Hell is written; asked why act, he said good deeds are made easy for the blessed.

    1362Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  35. 470

    Whoever swears falsely by another religion is as he said; whoever kills himself by iron, throttling or stabbing repeats it in Hell, and Paradise was forbidden to one who did.

    1363–1365Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)3
  36. 471

    Umar objected when the Prophet stood to pray over Abdullah bin Ubai; the Prophet said he had been given the choice and prayed, and soon verses forbade it.

    1366Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  37. 472

    When people praised a passing dead man the Prophet said Paradise was affirmed for him, and Hell when they spoke ill; even two Muslims testifying to piety suffice.

    1367–1368Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  38. 473

    The believer in his grave testifies to Allah and His Messenger; the Prophet addressed Badr's slain in the well, confirmed the grave's punishment and sought refuge from it.

    1369–1373Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  39. 474

    Two angels question the buried; Jews were heard punished in their graves; the Prophet sought refuge from that punishment; the dead are shown their place morning and evening.

    1374–1380Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)7
  40. 475

    A Muslim whose three children die before puberty enters Paradise; Ibrahim has a wet-nurse there; Allah knows what pagans' children would have done; every child is born with true faith.

    1381–1385Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)5
  41. 476

    In a dream Gabriel and Michael showed the Prophet the punishment of a liar, a neglecter of the Quran, adulterers and usurers, then Abraham with children and the martyrs' home.

    1386Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  42. 477

    Dying, Abu Bakr asked Aisha about the Prophet's shroud and the day he died, hoped to die that Monday, and asked to be shrouded in his worn garment.

    1387Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  43. 478

    The Prophet told a man whose mother died suddenly that she would be rewarded if he gave alms on her behalf.

    1388Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  44. 479

    The Prophet died in Aisha's lap and was buried in her house, his grave not made prominent lest it be prayed at; a foot later exposed there was Umar's.

    1389–1390.3Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)4
  45. 480

    Aisha chose burial in Al-Baqi, not beside the Prophet and his two companions; the stabbed Umar sought her leave to lie beside them and named six for the caliphate.

    1391–1392Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)2
  46. 481

    The Prophet said not to abuse the dead, for they have reached the result of what they sent forward.

    1393Funerals (Al-Janaa'iz)1
  47. 482

    When Abu Lahab said to the Prophet, "Perish you all the day," the revelation came: perish the hands of Abu Lahab, and perish he.
