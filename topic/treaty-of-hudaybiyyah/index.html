Treaty of Hudaybiyyah

14 verses · 4 flows · in 1 surah

#TopicVersesSurahCount
  1. 1350

    Revealed after Hudaybiyyah: Allah gave the Prophet a clear conquest, to forgive his past and future sins, complete His favor, guide him and aid him with mighty victory.

    48:1–3Al-Fath3
  2. 1354

    Those who pledged allegiance to the Prophet at Hudaybiyyah pledged to Allah, His hand over theirs; whoever breaks it harms himself, and whoever fulfills it earns great reward.

    48:10Al-Fath1
  3. 1356

    Allah was pleased with the believers who pledged allegiance under the tree at Hudaybiyyah, sent them tranquillity, and rewarded them with an imminent conquest, much booty and further victories.

    48:18–21Al-Fath4
  4. 1357

    Allah held back each side's hands within Makkah, where disbelievers barred believers from al-Masjid al-Haram; He sent tranquillity and showed His Messenger a true vision of entering it safely.

    48:22–27Al-Fath6
