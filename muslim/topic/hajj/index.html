Topic · Worship & character

Hajj

161 hadith · 22 flows · in 2 books

#TopicHadithBookCount
  1. 491

    Pilgrims took ihram for Umrah, Hajj or both, the Prophet for Hajj; menstruating Aisha was told to undo her hair, leave Umrah for Hajj, then do Umrah from Tan'im.

    2910–2921The Book of Pilgrimage12
  2. 492

    Those without sacrificial animals turned Hajj ihram into Umrah, the Prophet angered by their hesitation; Aisha, told her Tawaf sufficed, went with her brother to Tan'im; Safiyya could leave.

    2922–2936The Book of Pilgrimage15
  3. 493

    Jabir recalls those without sacrificial animals leaving ihram fully until Tarwiya, Aisha's menses, Suraqa told it was forever, and Umar later telling people to separate Hajj from Umrah.

    2937–2949The Book of Pilgrimage13
  4. 496

    Abu Musa left ihram after Tawaf and Sa'i, as told, and gave verdicts so until Umar, citing the Book and Sunnah, held otherwise, disliking pilgrims enjoying their wives before Hajj.

    2957–2961The Book of Pilgrimage5
  5. 497

    Uthman forbade Tamattu but Ali practised it; Abu Dharr called it, with temporary marriage, a concession for the Companions alone; Sa'd did it when Mu'awiya was still an unbeliever.

    2962–2971The Book of Pilgrimage10
  6. 498

    Imran b. Husain taught that the Prophet combined Hajj and Umrah, performing Tamattu; no verse abrogated it, he never forbade it, and one man's contrary view was personal opinion.

    2972–2981The Book of Pilgrimage10
  7. 499

    Ibn Umar describes the Prophet's Tamattu in the Farewell Pilgrimage; asked by Hafsa why he kept ihram, the Prophet said he had matted his hair and driven his sacrificial animal.

    2982–2988The Book of Pilgrimage7
  8. 500

    During Hajjaj's attack on Ibn Zubair, Ibn Umar bound himself to Umrah and Hajj together with one Tawaf for both; he and Anas differ on whether the Prophet combined them.

    2989–2996The Book of Pilgrimage8
  9. 502

    Asma left ihram having no sacrificial animal while Zubair kept his; she, Aisha and Zubair did Umrah then Hajj, and she confirms the Prophet permitted Tamattu, which Ibn Zubair forbade.

    3002–3008The Book of Pilgrimage7
  10. 503

    Pre-Islamic Arabs held Umrah in Hajj months a grave sin; the Prophet had those without animals turn Hajj ihram into Umrah, joined to Hajj forever, and a dream approved Tamattu.

    3009–3015The Book of Pilgrimage7
  11. 505

    Ibn Abbas held, as the Prophet's Sunnah and from verse 22:33, that circling the House frees a pilgrim from ihram; Mu'awiya says he clipped the Prophet's hair at al-Marwa.

    3018–3022The Book of Pilgrimage5
  12. 506

    Abu Sa'id recalls Hajj ihram changed to Umrah; Jabir says Umar forbade both Mut'as; Anas reports Ali's ihram matching the Prophet's and heard his Talbiya for Umrah and Hajj together.

    3023–3029The Book of Pilgrimage7
  13. 520

    The Prophet prayed repeatedly for those who shaved, then for those who clipped; after stoning and sacrificing he was shaved, right side first, the hair given out to people.

    3144–3155The Book of Pilgrimage12
  14. 521

    At Mina in the Farewell Pilgrimage, asked about shaving before sacrificing or sacrificing before stoning, the Prophet answered every question of order: do it, there is no harm.

    3156–3158The Book of Pilgrimage3
  15. 522

    Asked on the Day of Nahr about stoning, sacrificing, shaving or Tawaf al-Ifada done out of order, the Prophet saw no harm; he prayed noon at Mina after the Tawaf.

    3159–3165The Book of Pilgrimage7
  16. 523

    The Prophet prayed noon at Mina on Tarwiya and later halted at al-Abtah (Muhassab), where Quraysh pledged against Banu Hashim; Aisha says it is no Sunnah, just easy for departing.

    3166–3176The Book of Pilgrimage11
  17. 524

    Al-Abbas was allowed to spend Mina's nights in Mecca to water the pilgrims, and Ibn Abbas's family serves nabidh because the Prophet drank it there and approved.

    3177–3179The Book of Pilgrimage3
  18. 532

    A Khath'am woman may perform Hajj for her aged father who cannot ride, and a child lifted up to the Prophet is credited with Hajj, with reward for his mother.

    3251–3256The Book of Pilgrimage6
  19. 537

    The Prophet halted and prayed at the stony ground of Dhu'l-Hulaifa, where he was told at night it is blessed, and Ibn Umar halted there returning from Hajj or Umrah.

    3282–3286The Book of Pilgrimage5
  20. 538

    Abu Bakr, leading the Hajj before the Farewell Pilgrimage, sent criers on the Day of Nahr: after that year no polytheist may make Hajj nor naked person circle the House.

    3287The Book of Pilgrimage1
  21. 539

    Allah frees more servants from Hell on the Day of Arafa than any other; Umrah expiates sins until the next; accepted Hajj earns Paradise; pilgrims without indecency return sinless.

    3288–3293The Book of Pilgrimage6
  22. 1325

    When the Ansar on pilgrimage entered their houses only from the back, the verse 'Piety is not that you come to the doors from behind' was revealed.

    7549The Book of Commentary on the Qur'an1
