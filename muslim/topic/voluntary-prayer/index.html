Topic · Worship & character

Voluntary Prayer

68 hadith · 10 flows · in 1 book

#TopicHadithBookCount
  1. 271

    Aisha said the Prophet prayed the forenoon prayer only after journeys, or prayed four rak'ahs or more; Umm Hani saw him pray eight rak'ahs when Mecca was conquered.

    1660–1670The Book of Prayer - Travellers11
  2. 272

    Charity is due on every bone each morning and two forenoon rak'ahs suffice; Abu Huraira and Abu Darda were told to fast three days monthly, pray forenoon and witr.

    1671–1675The Book of Prayer - Travellers5
  3. 273

    The Prophet prayed two short rak'ahs between the call and iqama of the dawn prayer, kept them more than any voluntary prayer, and said they are better than the world.

    1676–1689The Book of Prayer - Travellers14
  4. 274

    In the two rak'ahs before the dawn prayer the Prophet recited 'Say: O unbelievers' and 'Say: Allah is One', or verses from al-Baqara and Al Imran.

    1690–1693The Book of Prayer - Travellers4
  5. 275

    Allah builds a house in Paradise for whoever prays twelve voluntary rak'ahs a day, and Ibn Umar listed those he prayed with the Prophet before and after the obligatory prayers.

    1694–1698The Book of Prayer - Travellers5
  6. 277

    In his last year or two the Prophet prayed most voluntary prayers sitting; he said a seated prayer is worth half, but he is not like others.

    1708–1716The Book of Prayer - Travellers9
  7. 283

    The Prophet said the prayer of the penitent is observed when young weaned camels feel the heat of the sun.

    1746–1747The Book of Prayer - Travellers2
  8. 298

    Pray some prayers at home and do not make houses graves; a man's prayer at home is best except the obligatory, and Satan flees a house where al-Baqara is recited.

    1820–1826The Book of Prayer - Travellers7
  9. 322

    Aisha said the Prophet never left two rak'ahs after Asr in her house; he told Umm Salama they made up the two after noon, missed while receiving a delegation.

    1931–1937The Book of Prayer - Travellers7
  10. 323

    People prayed two rak'ahs between the sunset call and Maghrib prayer, which the Prophet saw but neither ordered nor forbade; between adhan and iqamah is a prayer for whoever wishes.

    1938–1941The Book of Prayer - Travellers4
