    The hand is cut for no less than a quarter dinar or a shield's price; the Prophet invoked Allah's curse on a thief losing it for an egg or rope.

    When Usama interceded for a Makhzumi woman who stole, the Prophet rebuked him, saying earlier peoples perished by sparing the noble, and that he would cut even Fatima's hand.

    4410–4413The Book of Legal Punishments4
  3. 714

    Allah ordained that unmarried adulterers receive a hundred lashes and a year's exile and married ones a hundred lashes and stoning; Umar said the verse of stoning was sent down.

  4. 715

    Ma'iz b. Malik confessed adultery four times; after asking about his sanity and marriage the Prophet had him stoned, then threatened whoever lagged behind on expeditions bleating like a he-goat.

    4420–4430The Book of Legal Punishments11

    Ma'iz and women of Ghamid and Juhaina asked to be purified of adultery; the women were stoned after giving birth, and the Prophet prayed over them, praising their repentance.

    Jews guilty of adultery were stoned once the Torah's verse of stoning, which they had set aside, came out; Ibn Abu Aufa did not know whether stoning preceded Sura al-Nur.

    4437–4444The Book of Legal Punishments8
  7. 718

    A slave-woman who commits adultery is flogged without reproach and sold after repeated offences; Ali delayed flogging one who had just given birth, and the Prophet approved.
