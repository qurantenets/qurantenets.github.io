About Quran Flow

Quran Flow takes one or more consecutive verses and gives the passage a one or two word name that says what it is about, keeping the Quran's own order. Reading the names one after another shows the flow of a surah; reading every passage under one name shows what the Quran says on that subject.

How the flows are made

A flow is a run of consecutive verses on one subject. Its name comes from the words of the verses themselves. Every flow on this site has been read and approved by the project's editor before it appears here. Verses that are not grouped yet are shown as such, with a link to read them.

A name is a reading aid, not a translation or a commentary. Always read the verses themselves.
